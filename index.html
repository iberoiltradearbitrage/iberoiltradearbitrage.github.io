Business Lines

Three lines of business, one operating standard.

Each line is contracted separately and can be engaged on its own. What does not change between them is the method: verify first, document everything, and coordinate to completion.

01

Procurement

Sourcing of products, materials and industrial supplies for energy operations — from requirement definition to supplier qualification and purchase coordination.

  • Supplier identification and qualification
  • RFQ preparation and offer comparison
  • Purchase order coordination and follow-up
  • Inspection and acceptance documentation
02

Logistics Agency Services

Agency representation across the physical chain: storage, transport, inspection and delivery, with the documentation kept aligned to what was contractually agreed.

  • Milestone tracking through to discharge
03

  • Settlement facilitation between principals

How We Work

Four stages, in this order, without exception.

03

Organisational Philosophy

Why the company works the way it does.

Energy transactions are built on trust that has to be earned document by document. Our philosophy is a working method, not a statement of intent.

Mission

To make international fuel transactions executable — connecting qualified counterparties, sourcing what the operation requires, and holding the commercial, documentary and logistical coordination together from mandate to delivery.

Vision

To be the intermediary that European and international operators call when a transaction has to be done properly: verified counterparties, documentation that withstands scrutiny, and no surprises between signature and discharge.

Commitment

We take a limited number of mandates and we decline the ones that do not hold up. A transaction we turn down costs us a fee. A transaction we should have turned down costs our client far more.

Verification first

Nobody is taken at their word. Identity, ownership and capacity are checked before commitment.

Documentary rigour

What is agreed is what is written, and what is written is what is delivered.

Independence

We act for one principal at a time, and we disclose our role in every transaction.

Discretion

Client information stays with the client. Nothing circulates without written authorisation.

Our People

A small team, deliberately.

IBER OIL is run by professionals with direct experience in energy trading, procurement and logistics coordination. The company is intentionally compact: the person who takes your enquiry is the person who coordinates your transaction, and the file does not change hands as it moves.

Our teams work from Spain, the United States and Japan. That spread is operational, not decorative — it covers the European, Ibero-American and Asian working day, which is where fuel transactions actually stall when nobody is awake to answer. Business is conducted in Spanish and English throughout.

Governance

How we are set up, plainly stated.

A Spanish sociedad de responsabilidad limitada with its registered office in Madrid, operating under Spanish commercial regulation and European Union law. Wholly owned by its founder and director — no nominees, no intermediate holdings.

We act as a commercial intermediary. We do not hold, control or transmit client funds, and we are not a financial institution, an exchange or a payment service provider.

Counterparty checks Identity, beneficial ownership and sanctions screening before onboarding, reviewed periodically.
Regulatory framework Spanish Law 10/2010 and Directive (EU) 2015/849 govern how we verify and record.

Role disclosure Our capacity as agent is stated in writing in every mandate we accept.
